Drone sprayers

Drones that spray crops from the air, covering a field in minutes with less chemical, less water and no contact for the worker.

They fly a planned route over the field and spray pesticide, fertiliser or nutrients evenly. They suit paddy, cotton, sugarcane and orchards. Pilots need training and a registered drone under the national drone rules.
